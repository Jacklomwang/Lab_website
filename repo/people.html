Director

Georgios D. Mitsis

Associate Professor & William Dawson Scholar · McGill University

Georgios leads the Biosignals & Systems Analysis Lab, working at the intersection of systems engineering and physiology. His research develops nonlinear, data-driven modelling methods and applies them to cerebral hemodynamics, computational oncology and glucose regulation. (edit this bio)
