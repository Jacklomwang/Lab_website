About the Lab

Our Mission

The Biosignals & Systems Analysis Lab develops data-driven mathematical models of the body's dynamic systems. Our mission is to enhance the understanding and application of physiological signals — fMRI, heart rate, blood pressure and EEG — enabling breakthroughs that improve lives. We foster innovation and collaboration across engineering, computer science, neuroscience and medicine.
